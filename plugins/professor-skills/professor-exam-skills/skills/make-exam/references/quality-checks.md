# Exam quality checks

Use the smallest relevant subset. A single item does not need an exam-level
audit; a high-stakes multi-variant final does.

## Item checks

- The prompt asks for observable evidence, not a vague topic dump.
- All necessary information is supplied or legitimately expected knowledge.
- Wording has one defensible interpretation.
- The item can be solved as written.
- Marks reflect substantive steps.
- The key answers the exact prompt.
- The rubric permits valid alternative reasoning.
- Dependent subparts define whether error-carried-forward applies.
- Selected-response distractors correspond to plausible misconceptions.

## Exam checks

- Marks sum exactly to the stated total.
- Estimated minutes fit the duration with reading/review buffer.
- Outcome weights match the blueprint.
- No essential outcome is accidentally omitted or duplicated.
- The mix samples the intended performance range.
- Optional questions do not create easier routes to the same grade.
- Instructions define resources, rounding, units, attempts, and submission.
- The student paper contains no hidden answer, rubric, or variant metadata.

## Variant checks

- Each instance is independently solved.
- Reasoning decisions and scaffolding are invariant.
- Numeric values avoid accidental edge cases.
- Reading, computation, and interpretation loads are comparable.
- The same rubric gives the same meaning to scores across variants.
- Variant identity is recoverable for grading without exposing answers.

## Fairness and integrity checks

- The exam measures intended competence rather than avoidable speed, language,
  formatting, or technology barriers.
- Accommodations do not invalidate the targeted evidence.
- The AI and resource policy is explicit.
- Integrity controls are proportionate to credible threats.
- Detector output is never treated as proof by itself.

## Validation language

Use precise claims:

- **checked:** internally reviewed against these rules;
- **reviewed:** examined by a named human reviewer;
- **piloted:** attempted by representative students;
- **analyzed:** item or score data were evaluated.

Never substitute one term for another.
